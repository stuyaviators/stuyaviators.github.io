POSITIONS

Meet the positions within StuyAviators and see how each contributes to the club’s projects, events, and overall success.
Trainee Pilot (Pre-Vice President)
Very High Commitment
Responsibilities
  • Train under current leadership to learn club operations and management.
  • Prepare to step into the role of Vice President and potentially President.
  • Assist in planning and overseeing club events, projects, and initiatives.
  • Support other leaders and help ensure smooth daily operations.
  • Dedicate significant time and effort to help the club grow.
  • Demonstrate readiness and initiative to take on higher leadership roles when appropriate.
Prerequisites
  • Strong commitment and willingness to dedicate substantial time.
  • Interest in leadership development (path toward VP/President).
  • Prior experience in the club recommended but not required.
Flight Engineer (Secretary)
High Commitment
Responsibilities
  • Support the leadership team in running the club.
  • Step in to lead if the Vice Presidents or President are absent.
  • Partially shadow the duties of the President to gain insight into club management and decision-making.
  • Take on all responsibilities of a Flight Attendant, plus additional tasks as they arise throughout the school year.
  • Demonstrate commitment and dedication to helping the club succeed.
Prerequisites
  • Reliability and consistency in attendance.
  • Strong organizational and time management skills.
  • Willingness to shadow leadership and step in when needed.
Flight Navigator (Coordinations)
Medium Commitment
Responsibilities
  • Manage club meetings and track attendance to ensure sessions run smoothly.
  • Plan future meetings and agendas to keep the club organized and on schedule.
  • Maintain the club’s Epsilon page with up-to-date information.
  • Update other platforms with meeting details, including the #meetings channel on Discord.
Prerequisites
  • Ability to stay organized and manage schedules.
  • Familiarity with Epsilon (or willingness to learn quickly).
  • Clear communication for posting updates.
Radio Operator (Communications)
Medium Commitment
Responsibilities
  • Promote the club through social media platforms (Instagram, Facebook) and around the school with leaflets and flyers.
  • Announce upcoming meetings and events to increase participation.
  • Boost engagement and maximize the club’s presence online to reach a wider audience.
  • Create eye-catching graphics for both online and offline promotion.
Prerequisites
  • Strong design and communication skills.
  • Familiarity with social media platforms (Instagram, Facebook).
  • Understanding of digital engagement and promotion strategies.
Aircraft Mechanic (Finance)
Low Commitment
Responsibilities
  • Manage the club’s assets, including items owned by the club and those borrowed from members or other sources.
  • Organize fundraisers and track expenses to ensure proper use of funds.
  • Collaborate with other departments to allocate resources effectively.
  • Oversee club purchases and maintain accurate inventory and budget records using spreadsheets.
  • Communicate with faculty advisors or external organizations to request invoices, inquire about pricing, or place orders.
Prerequisites
  • Comfortable working with spreadsheets and budgets.
  • Strong organizational and record-keeping skills.
  • Communication skills for coordinating purchases and invoices.
Flight Technician (IT)
Medium Commitment
Responsibilities
  • Create, maintain, and update the club’s website.
  • Troubleshoot technical issues and ensure digital tools run smoothly.
  • Explore and integrate better platforms and tools to improve workflow efficiency.
  • Maintain and streamline access to club resources.
  • Apply knowledge of front-end development (HTML, CSS) and familiarity with frameworks and GitHub.
Prerequisites
  • Prior or current enrollment in Annual CS or APCSA.
  • Experience with front-end development (HTML & CSS minimum).
  • Familiarity with frameworks and GitHub preferred.
Air Traffic Controller (Outreach)
Medium Commitment
Responsibilities
  • Contact organizations and invite guest speakers, including current and former pilots, to Stuyvesant.
  • Research and share information about upcoming aviation events in the New York/New Jersey area.
  • Coordinate with the faculty advisor when needed.
  • Prior outreach experience is strongly recommended for this role.
Prerequisites
  • Strong communication and networking skills.
  • Prior outreach or leadership experience recommended.
  • Professionalism when contacting external organizations.
Flight Attendant (Helper)
Low Commitment
Responsibilities
  • Assist coordinators and the leadership team at events, including Clubs & Pubs and regular meetings.
  • Set a positive example for other members and represent the club well.
  • Actively contribute to the club’s activities and initiatives.
  • Support the smooth running of events and day-to-day operations.
Prerequisites
  • Enthusiasm and reliability.
  • Willingness to assist at events and meetings.
  • Positive attitude and ability to set an example for others.
First Class (Expert)
Very Low Commitment
Responsibilities
  • Share strong knowledge in a specific area of aviation.
  • Create and deliver presentations to the club community.
  • Build skills in public speaking, confidence, and teaching.
  • Contribute to the club’s educational mission by sharing expertise with members.
Achievements
  • Strong knowledge in a specific area of aviation.
  • Confidence in public speaking and teaching.
  • Preparedness to create presentations and share knowledge.
