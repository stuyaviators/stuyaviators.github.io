ABOUT PROJECT SKYLAB

Project Skylab's name takes inspiration from the United States' first space station, Skylab, which was launched by NASA in May 1973 and was in operation until July 1979.        

Project Skylab aims to inspire future generations to explore aviation, aeronautics, and aerospace. By providing free access to flight simulators, immersive educational resources, and hands-on experiences, Skylab sparks curiosity and fosters a love for the science behind flight — regardless of background or prior experience.

Beyond simulation, Project Skylab engages students with community-led presentations, school trips, and aviation-focused activities, while advocating for formal Aviation or Aerospace Engineering electives at Stuyvesant High School. Our mission is simple: make aviation education inclusive, accessible, and inspiring.

IMPACT & BENEFITS

Educational Enrichment

Project Skylab enhances students’ learning by providing hands-on aviation experiences and interactive simulations that bring STEM concepts to life. By connecting classroom knowledge with real-world applications, students gain a deeper understanding of aerodynamics, physics, and aviation systems. This foundation prepares them for future studies and opportunities in aerospace and related fields.

Skill Development

Through community presentations, interactive activities, and leadership opportunities, Project Skylab helps students build critical skills beyond aviation knowledge. Participants develop public speaking, teamwork, and problem-solving abilities while engaging with peers who share their interests. These experiences cultivate confidence and initiative, which carry over into academics and future pursuits.

Inclusivity & Accessibility

Project Skylab is committed to making aviation education open and welcoming to all students, regardless of background, gender, or experience. By providing free access to simulators and resources, the program ensures everyone has the opportunity to explore flight. This inclusive approach fosters a diverse community united by curiosity, passion, and a love for aviation.

Career Pathways

Project Skylab introduces students to the exciting world of aviation and aerospace, inspiring them to consider rewarding careers in these industries. Exposure to flight simulation, guest speakers, and educational resources allows students to explore potential career paths early. By fostering passion and confidence, Skylab helps students envision themselves as future pilots, engineers, or aerospace professionals.

OBJECTIVES

Project Skylab is designed as a step-by-step journey to bring aviation education to life at Stuyvesant High School. Each stage builds on the last, starting with community-building, moving to expanded engagement, and culminating in the full implementation of a flight simulator and aviation curriculum. By breaking the project into stages, Project Skylab ensures a sustainable, inclusive, and impactful approach that allows students to explore, learn, and grow at every step.

The first stage of Project Skylab focused on creating a strong foundation: a welcoming aviation community at Stuyvesant High School. Through weekly club meetings, students are introduced to flight simulators, interactive games, educational videos, and engaging presentations. This stage is all about sparking interest, connecting like-minded peers, and cultivating a shared passion for aviation.

Stage Two is centered on growth and enrichment. The club expands its reach by hosting guest speakers, organizing school trips, and enhancing the flight simulator experience. Leadership structures are strengthened, new activities are introduced, and funding is pursued to support upgraded equipment and a more immersive educational environment. This stage ensures that Project Skylab moves beyond introductory experiences toward meaningful engagement.

The final stage brings the full vision of Project Skylab to life. A full-sized flight simulator, nicknamed the “Skylab,” is commissioned, and the club advocates for formal Aviation and Aerospace electives at Stuyvesant. This stage ensures that aviation education is accessible, sustainable, and inspiring for future students, creating pathways into aviation and aerospace careers.

01.

Establish a thriving aviation community where curiosity and passion for flight can take root. 

02.

Grow the club’s impact by offering advanced experiences, guest speakers, and more hands-on learning opportunities.

03.

Deliver a permanent flight simulation device and advocate for aviation education at Stuyvesant High School.
