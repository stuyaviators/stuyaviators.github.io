THE FOUNDATION OF OUR CLUB

StuyAviators’ club charter serves as the foundation for our community. It outlines our mission, the structure of our meetings, leadership appointment procedures, and the principles that make our club unique. This document ensures transparency, consistency, and a shared understanding of how we collaborate, grow, and pursue our passion for aviation.

OUR PURPOSE

Stuyvesant High School’s official aviation club is committed to making the world of aviation, aeronautics, and aerospace accessible, inclusive, and inspiring for all students—regardless of background, identity, level of experience, or socioeconomic status. Through engaging, student-driven experiences and a strong emphasis on mentorship and exploration, we strive to empower members to take initiative, build confidence, and develop both technical and leadership skills.

Our flagship initiative, Project Skylab, aims to inspire current and future generations to pursue careers in aviation and aerospace by immersing them in the world of aviation through hands-on learning, flight simulations, guest speakers, and real-world case studies, enhancing their education and deepening their knowledge.

Students are encouraged to explore a wide range of career paths—from piloting, drone operation, and air traffic control to aerial firefighting, search and rescue, and service in the United States Air Force—as well as fields like aerospace engineering, aeronautical engineering, aviation technology, astronautical engineering, avionics, mechanical engineering, and other related disciplines that drive innovation in flight and space exploration.

StuyAviators seeks to spark curiosity and foster a love for flight within the Stuyvesant community by cultivating a welcoming space for members to grow as learners, presenters, and community leaders—bringing aviation to life at Stuyvesant and making the skies more inclusive and accessible to all. 

WHEN WE MEET

Wednesday, Thursday, Friday

SCHEDULE OVERVIEW

Meetings are typically held once or twice a week, usually on Wednesdays, Thursdays, or Fridays, with additional sessions scheduled based on interest and demand.

During less academically intense periods, meeting frequency may increase, subject to leadership availability.

Conversely, during particularly demanding times—such as SING! season or AP Exam Month—meetings may be reduced or temporarily paused to allow members and leaders to prioritize their academic commitments.

StuyAviators is a low-commitment club designed to complement, not compete with, students’ academic responsibilities. Participation in club activities should not interfere with your academic performance.

Occasionally, we may host 'Relax, Unwind, Simulate, Hangout' (RUSH) events—casual drop-in sessions featuring activities like flight simulator demonstrations—providing members with a fun, low-pressure opportunity to relax and recharge.

TYPICAL MEETING STRUCTURE

Most meetings run for approximately 75 minutes—from the end of school until 5:00 PM. Longer sessions may be held depending on the topic and activity planned.

The content of each meeting varies by meeting type, which rotates regularly based on student interest and demand. StuyAviators hosts a wide range of meeting types and activities, including but not limited to: Flight Simulator Demonstrations (FSD), Community-Led Presentations (CLP), Plane Crash Investigation Labs (PCI), Aviation Challenges & Competitions, Guest Speaker Events, and Group Trips. Recent additions include Student-Led Courses (e.g. General Aviation, Drone Basics, Learning to Fly) and Air Traffic Control Simulations (ATC).

LEADERSHIP APPOINTMENT PROCEDURES

Our sophisticated appointment procedures are crucial to the successful operation of StuyAviators. Leadership team members—collectively known as the Flight Crew—are selected carefully and deliberately to ensure the club runs smoothly across all areas.

Helpers are volunteers appointed on a rolling basis to assist with ongoing club operations and support tasks.

Department Heads, who lead the club’s various departments, are appointed biannually through an application process released at the beginning of each semester.

Prospective Vice Presidents may also apply through this process, but may alternatively be handpicked by the President based on merit, leadership, and commitment.

The position of President is passed down to two Co-Presidents—typically the most senior and experienced members of the leadership team (usually Vice Presidents from the next graduating class)—when the current President or Co-Presidents are set to graduate, ensuring continuity and shared responsibility at the highest level of leadership.

Our well-structured disciplinary procedures are essential to maintaining the safety, respect, and well-being of all members. By attending any StuyAviators meeting or event, members agree to follow all rules and policies set forth by StuyAviators, the Stuyvesant High School Student Union, Stuyvesant High School, and the New York City Department of Education (NYC DOE).

Infractions are tracked, and repeated violations may result in disciplinary actions, including temporary suspension or blacklisting from future meetings. In extreme cases, issues will be escalated to appropriate school personnel, and further actions may be taken in consultation with school administration.

WHAT MAKES US UNIQUE

StuyAviators stands as Stuyvesant’s premier hub for aviation and aerospace exploration—powered entirely by student initiative, fueled by curiosity, and grounded in collaboration. What sets us apart is not just our passion for flight, but our commitment to building a sustainable and accessible aviation ecosystem within our school.

At the heart of this mission is Project Skylab, a comprehensive framework designed to bring as much aviation to Stuyvesant as possible. It outlines a strategic roadmap: from securing funding for a professional flight simulator setup to launching student-led aviation courses, and expanding field trip opportunities to increasing community engagement. Project Skylab embodies our long-term vision to democratize aviation education by offering hands-on experiences, structured learning pathways, and centralized resources—including free access to advanced simulation software like X-Plane 12 and FAA-certified flight training devices—all in one permanent space open to all.

StuyAviators is not just a casual hobby club—it’s a launchpad for both curious beginners and aspiring aviation professionals. Whether it’s creating simple paper airplanes or tackling landing challenges with professional-grade simulators, StuyAviators offers a welcoming space for anyone who shares a love of flight.

StuyAviators also offers a wide and ever-evolving lineup of hands-on, student-led experiences—all designed and refined by students, for students. Whether you’re brand new to aviation or a seasoned enthusiast, we believe that anyone should be able to experience the thrill of flight and explore the science behind it—without needing expensive equipment, prior experience, or formal training. Activities range from crafting planes out of paper or styrofoam and experimenting with gliders, to modeling aircraft in 3D design software and printing them, and much more. We actively encourage the exploration of any aviation-related idea as long as there is interest and demand.

Through one of our mottos, "For the community, by the community," we empower members to lead presentations, design workshops, host guest speakers, and plan trips, all while shaping a club culture rooted in accessibility, inclusivity, and innovation. Our members don’t just participate—they build, teach, explore, and lead.

Aviation didn’t formally exist at Stuyvesant—so we built the space for it.
