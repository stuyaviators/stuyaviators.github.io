OUR STORY

A brief history of who we are and how we began.

Learn More

Founded on November 19, 2022

StuyAviators was founded by Jerry Qiu ('26) and William Vongphanith ('23) to unite students passionate about aviation and provide a space to explore the industry. What began as a small group of hobbyists quickly grew into a community where students could share ideas, learn from one another, and connect over a love of flight.

The club’s creation marked the first time Stuyvesant had a dedicated space for aviation enthusiasts, opening doors for students to learn, experiment, and dream beyond the classroom.

FROM HOBBY TO INITIATIVE

In its early days, StuyAviators focused on casual meetings and online discussions, but the vision quickly grew. Membership expanded, leadership roles were introduced, and the club began hosting structured meetings to provide hands-on experiences and mentorship. This period laid the groundwork for a broader mission: not just to gather aviation enthusiasts, but to actively inspire and educate the entire student body about the world of flight.

LAUNCHING PROJECT SKYLAB

January 8, 2025: Our first guest speaker event, featuring Alvin Arjoon.

In the 2024-25 school year, StuyAviators reached a new milestone with the launch of Project Skylab, the club’s flagship initiative.

With guidance from faculty advisor Mr. Dillon, the club acquired professional-grade flight simulation software and realistic controls, giving students hands-on aviation experiences unlike any before.

Designed to be accessible to all members, Project Skylab allows students to explore aviation, build technical skills, and discover potential career pathways in aerospace, reflecting StuyAviators’ commitment to learning, inclusion, and inspiring the next generation of pilots and aviation enthusiasts.

With this milestone, the club’s vision became clearer than ever. StuyAviators is no longer just a gathering of aviation enthusiasts—it is a fully realized community dedicated to education, innovation, and accessibility.

Project Skylab set the foundation for future growth, ambitious initiatives, and opportunities for every member to soar beyond the classroom and into the world of aviation.
