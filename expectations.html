CODE OF CONDUCT

As members of StuyAviators at Stuyvesant High School, all participants are expected to uphold the highest standards of conduct to maintain a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment. Membership implies agreement to the following expectations:
  1. Respect for All. Members shall treat peers, club leadership, faculty advisors, and guests with courtesy, professionalism, and consideration at all times. Interactions should be constructive, and conflicts should be resolved respectfully.
  2. Prohibition of Malicious Humor. Members must refrain from making jokes, comments, or references to historical events in a malicious or harmful manner, and must not engage in any illegal, unsafe, or inappropriate actions, such as intentionally damaging equipment or misusing simulators.
  3. Commitment to Inclusivity. All members should strive to create a welcoming environment, encouraging participation from students of all grades, backgrounds, and experience levels. Discriminatory or exclusionary behavior is unacceptable.
  4. Responsible Use of Resources. All club equipment, including flight simulators, computers, and other materials, must be used responsibly and with care. Members are expected to follow safety protocols and handle shared resources properly.
  5. Collaboration and Cooperation. Members should actively support their peers, contribute positively to projects and group activities, and help foster a collaborative, team-oriented club environment.
  6. Respectful Communication. All communication, whether verbal, written, or online (including emails, Discord, and other platforms), should remain respectful, professional, and appropriate at all times.
  7. Adherence to Leadership and Advisor Guidance. Members are expected to follow the guidance of the Flight Crew and faculty advisors, understanding that their leadership is essential for the club’s smooth and effective operation.
  8. Privacy and Consent. By participating in StuyAviators activities, members grant the club leadership permission to take photos and videos for documentation, promotion, or official purposes. However, members are not permitted to record, photograph, or share other members’ work or personal information without explicit consent.
  9. Dedication to Learning. Members are encouraged to approach all aviation activities, workshops, and discussions with curiosity, diligence, and a commitment to personal growth, skill development, and exploration of aviation.
  10. Compliance with Institutional Policies. All members agree to abide by the rules, regulations, and guidelines established by the Stuyvesant Student Union, Stuyvesant High School, and the New York City Department of Education. These standards take precedence over club policies and must be respected at all times.

ATTENDANCE POLICY

StuyAviators maintains a flexible approach to attendance. General members are not required to attend meetings regularly and are welcome to join whenever their schedules allow. Members may arrive or leave at any time during a session, though remaining for the full duration is encouraged in order to benefit fully from planned activities and discussions.

Club leadership, however, is expected to attend consistently. Regular participation by leaders ensures that meetings run smoothly and that projects and initiatives receive proper support. Expectations for leadership attendance are managed within the leadership team.

This policy allows members to engage at their own pace while ensuring stability and accountability among the club’s leadership.

MEETING ETIQUETTE

StuyAviators meetings are designed to be welcoming, engaging, and productive. To ensure a positive experience for all participants, the following expectations apply:
  • Attendance Recording. Attendance is taken within the first 15 minutes of each meeting. If you arrive late, please notify a Flight Crew member so your attendance can be noted.
  • Meeting Cancellations. If no members arrive within 20 minutes of a scheduled start time, the meeting will be cancelled.
  • Punctuality and Courtesy. Members should make every effort to arrive on time and remain until the meeting concludes. If you must leave early, do so quietly and without disrupting the session.
  • Conduct and Behavior. All participants are expected to follow the Code of Conduct. Disruptive, disrespectful, or inappropriate behavior may result in removal from the meeting.

CANCELLATIONS & RESCHEDULING

While StuyAviators strives to maintain a consistent and reliable meeting schedule, there may be times when meetings must be cancelled, postponed, or adjusted. Cancellations may occur for a variety of reasons, including but not limited to leadership availability, unexpected school events, inclement weather, or the sensitivity of planned activities in light of recent real-world aviation incidents (for example, postponing a crash investigation activity if an actual crash has recently taken place).

Whenever possible, members will be notified of a cancellation or rescheduling at least 12 hours before the scheduled start time. Notices will be sent via the club’s official communication channels (e.g., Discord, email, or Epsilon). If no members of the leadership team are available to attend, the meeting will be cancelled or rescheduled, and members will be informed promptly.

Cancelled meetings may be rescheduled for a later date or substituted with an alternative activity, depending on the nature of the cancellation. Members are encouraged to stay flexible and understanding, as cancellations and adjustments are made with respect to both member experience and broader circumstances.
