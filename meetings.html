TYPES OF MEETINGS

Discover our six core meeting types, along with two pilot sessions currently being tested to expand our offerings.
Flight Simulator Demonstrations (FSD)
Experience flight, anytime, anywhere!

Step into a professional flight simulator and experience the thrill of piloting from takeoff to landing. Members practice controlling aircraft, navigating using real-world procedures, and responding to various in-flight scenarios. These sessions build practical skills, improve situational awareness, and give aspiring aviators a safe, hands-on environment to explore the art and science of flying.

Community-Led Presentations (CLP)
For the community, by the community!

Members create and deliver presentations on aviation topics they’re passionate about, from the history of flight to cutting-edge technology. These sessions foster knowledge sharing, public speaking, and critical thinking, while encouraging collaboration and discussion among peers. CLPs give students the opportunity to lead, teach, and inspire the club community with their expertise and unique perspectives.

Plane Crash Investigation Labs (PCI)
Think like an NTSB investigator!

Dive deep into real-world aviation incidents as you analyze evidence, reconstruct events, and uncover the factors that led to each accident. Through hands-on investigations, members learn the techniques and methodologies used by professional accident investigators, gaining a unique perspective on flight safety, decision-making, and problem-solving in aviation.

Aviation Challenges & Competitions
Test your skills, compete, and soar!

Engage in interactive challenges and competitions that put your aviation knowledge and problem-solving skills to the test. From teamwork-based flight simulations to timed aviation quizzes and design challenges, these sessions encourage friendly competition, creativity, and critical thinking. Members can apply what they’ve learned in previous meetings, sharpen their skills, and experience the thrill of tackling real-world aviation scenarios in a fun, hands-on way.

Guest Speaker Events
Learn from professionals!

Meet and engage with aviation professionals, including pilots, engineers, Air Force mentors, and industry leaders. These sessions provide firsthand insights into careers, emerging technologies, and real-world aviation experiences. Members can ask questions, gain valuable advice, and connect with experts, bridging the gap between classroom learning and the professional world of aviation.

Group Trips
Learning beyond the classroom!

Join trips to airports, airshows, museums, and flight academies to experience aviation up close. These excursions provide hands-on learning opportunities, a chance to observe professionals in action, and a unique perspective on the industry. Members gain real-world exposure, build connections, and see how concepts from club meetings translate into the broader world of flight.

[NEW] Air Traffic Control Simulation (ATC)
Coordinate, communicate, conquer the airspace.

Experience the high-stakes world of air traffic control in a simulated environment. Members learn to manage airspace, communicate with pilots, and coordinate safe takeoffs, landings, and flight paths. These sessions build critical thinking, multitasking, and communication skills while giving students a realistic look at how ATC professionals keep the skies safe and efficient.

[NEW] Student-Led Courses (SLC)
Get a head start on flight training and theory!

Dive into in-depth courses taught by fellow members on topics like General Aviation, Drone Basics, and Learning to Fly. These multi-session classes allow students to explore subjects more deeply, practice hands-on skills, and gain knowledge directly from peers who are passionate and experienced in the field. Student-Led Courses foster collaboration, leadership, and a deeper understanding of aviation concepts.
