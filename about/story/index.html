OUR STORY

Founded on November 19, 2022, StuyAviators was created by Jerry Qiu ('26) and William Vongphanith ('23) to unite students with a passion for aviation and provide a comprehensive introduction to the industry. With its launch, Stuyvesant gained its first club fully dedicated to aviation exploration.



What began as a small hobby group quickly evolved into a growing community. Initially, StuyAviators served as a space to make friends and share common interests. While early activity was limited to casual chats on Discord, the foundation was laid for something much greater.



By the end of the 2022-2023 school year, StuyAviators had grown to around 40 members.



The 2023-2024 school year marked a turning point. The club expanded rapidly after participating in the Fall Clubs & Pubs Fair, reaching around 70 members. Leadership applications opened for the first time, and two new Vice Presidents—Matthew Lin ('26) and Andrew Zhang ('27)—joined the team following William's graduation. With renewed direction, StuyAviators began to shift toward a broader mission: to inspire and educate students about aviation.



Regular meetings began, and the launch of the @stuyaviators Instagram page helped promote the club and document its growth.



In the fall of the 2024-25 school year, StuyAviators solidified its identity. Mr. Dillon joined as the club's faculty advisor, enabling the club to seek funding through the Student Union and Parents' Association. Most significantly, this year marked the launch of Project Skylab, the club's flagship initiative to make aviation more inclusive and accessible to all students. In December, the club secured funding for professional-grade flight simulation software: X-Plane 12 and a basic set of realistic flight controls.



In January 2025, Alexander Smelyansky ('27) joined the leadership team as Vice President, helping to guide StuyAviators into its next chapter.



Project Skylab proved transformative. By April 2025, membership had surpassed 100 students. The club hosted its first guest speaker, Alvin Arjoon (Aviation High School '24), who shared his journey and advice on getting involved in aviation. That spring, StuyAviators also requested funding for a Cessna Basic Aviation Training Device (BATD) from RealSimGear—set to become the club's centerpiece, offering members the rare and thrilling once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to experience piloting a plane, completely free of charge.
