OUR MISSION

Project Skylab aims to inspire future generations to pursue careers in aviation, aeronautics, and aerospace. Careers in these fields are not only intellectually stimulating but also offer rewarding salaries and job security, particularly in light of the global pilot shortage. By providing free access to professional-grade flight simulators and immersive educational resources, our project seeks to spark curiosity among aviation enthusiasts and foster a love for the science behind flight within the Stuyvesant community, no matter what one's background is.

In addition to hands-on flying experiences, engaging community-led presentations on various topics, and free educational school trips offered the host club, StuyAviators, and funded by the school, Project Skylab aims to collaborate with school administration to introduce Aviation and/or Aerospace Engineering as a formal science elective offered at Stuyvesant High School, making the world of aviation more accessible to students who wish to explore these topics in greater depth.

Through this collaborative initiative, Project Skylab strive to make aviation education inclusive and widespread, opening pathways for students to consider future studies and careers in aviation and aerospace industries.
