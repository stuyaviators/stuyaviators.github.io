STORIES WE'VE SHARED

Stories previously spotlighted, showcasing our members’ journeys and experiences.
Yunus Gubrud
Stuyvesant High School Class of 2028

Yunus has always had a curiosity for aviation, though it started in unexpected ways. In middle school, he experimented with flight-based video games, flying heavy bombers across virtual skies just for the thrill of it. That early fascination grew when his stepdad, a private pilot, gifted him Microsoft Flight Simulator, sparking an obsession with learning procedures and flying for hours on end. After two years of virtual flight, Yunus took an introductory lesson at Princeton Flying School, which led him to begin formal flight training. Balancing school and flying has been challenging, so he’s currently pausing lessons with plans to continue in the summer. Looking ahead, Yunus hopes to earn his private pilot’s license at 17 and work toward additional ratings, believing that passion and curiosity are the most important keys to success in aviation.

Jerry Qiu
Stuyvesant High School Class of 2026

Jerry has transformed a lifelong curiosity about aviation into a serious pursuit. His interest began with flight simulator apps and online aviation content, eventually growing through more realistic simulators and hands-on experience. Jerry founded StuyAviators to share his passion and build a community for students interested in aviation. He has participated in real-world flight training, logging hours in Cessna aircraft and completing ground school at a U.S. Air Force-affiliated program, and is now preparing for his first solo. Balancing school and flight training presents challenges, but Jerry hopes to earn his Private Pilot License in the coming years and pursue a career in aviation, potentially through the Air Force.
